# Household Cooking & LPG Gas Conservation

## LPG Cylinder Emissions
A standard 14.2 kg Liquefied Petroleum Gas (LPG) cylinder generates approximately 42.5 kg to 47 kg of CO2 equivalent emissions upon combustion.

## Thermal Efficiency & Cooking Habits
- **Pressure Cooker Utilization**: Using pressure cookers rather than open pans cooks food up to 70% faster and reduces gas consumption by 50%.
- **Pre-soaking Grains & Pulses**: Pre-soaking lentils, rice, and beans for 30 minutes prior to boiling cuts cooking duration and gas usage by 20-30%.
- **Lid Coverage**: Keeping cooking pots covered prevents heat dissipation, reducing energy usage by up to 20%.

## Induction Cooktops & Renewable Transition
- **Induction Stove Efficiency**: Electric induction cooktops transfer roughly 85-90% of heat directly to cookware compared to only 40-55% thermal efficiency for gas flames.
- **Solar-Powered Cooking**: Pairing induction appliances with rooftop solar power completely decouples cooking heat from fossil fuel combustion.
