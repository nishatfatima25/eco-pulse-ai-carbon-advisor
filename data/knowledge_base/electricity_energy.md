# Electricity & Household Energy Efficiency

## Grid Carbon Intensity
Electricity generation relying on coal and thermal power plants emits roughly 0.82 kg of CO2 per kilowatt-hour (kWh). Reducing household electricity consumption directly prevents high grid emissions.

## Appliance Efficiency & Ratings
- **BEE 5-Star Appliances**: Standard air conditioners, refrigerators, and washing machines with 5-star energy efficiency ratings consume 25-30% less power than non-rated models.
- **LED Lighting Transition**: Replacing incandescent bulbs with LED lamps reduces lighting energy consumption by up to 80% while lasting 15 times longer.
- **Air Conditioner Inverter Technology & Setpoints**: Setting air conditioners to 24°C - 26°C instead of 18°C saves 6% electricity per degree raised. Inverter compressors adjust speed dynamically, avoiding peak start-up current spikes.

## Standby Power & Smart Management
- **Vampire Loads**: Electronics on standby mode (TVs, chargers, microwave clocks) account for 5% to 10% of residential power bills. Using smart power strips or switching off wall outlets stops idle consumption.
- **Rooftop Solar PV Systems**: Installing a 3 kW residential rooftop solar photovoltaic system reduces household emissions by approximately 3.5 to 4 metric tons of CO2 per year.
